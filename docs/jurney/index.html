Privacy Policy

Jurney - Last updated: 2026-11-08

Jurney ("the application") is an offline, local-first quest journal for tracking real-life adventures and bucket lists.

Data collection

Jurney has no account, app-operated backend or online community. The publisher does not receive your journal, profile, quest or memory content.

Data storage

Quest journals, custom quests, private notes and memories, planned dates, rituals, completion history, XP, badges, avatar and optional adventurer name are stored locally using Android system preferences. Language, theme, onboarding and reminder settings are also stored on the device.

Optional Android home screen widgets display locally generated quest content and progress. Widget content can be visible to anyone using your unlocked home screen. The widgets do not contact a server.

Android may include application data in system backup or device transfer according to your device and Google account settings. Jurney does not operate or have access to that backup.

Data transmission

  • Jurney does not automatically upload your data or make requests to an app-operated server.
  • Journal and quest features work offline. Sharing, support and opening the Play Store are explicit external actions; the receiving application or service has its own privacy practices.

Analytics and tracking

  • No analytics are used.
  • No tracking or advertising identifiers are used.
  • No third-party tracking libraries are included.

Data sharing

Sharing is always requested by the user through Android's native share sheet. Depending on the action, a shared summary may contain quest or journal titles and descriptions, aggregate weekly progress, achievements or profile information. Including the adventurer name in a profile share is optional. Private memory notes, journal notes and planned dates are excluded from these summary shares.

A separate JSON journal export copies the selected journal's data, including its notes and quest fields, to the clipboard. It is not the same as a summary share. Other applications may read clipboard content; review the export before sending it. Jurney does not upload exports. Recipients may retain or forward content under their own privacy policies, and deleting a local journal does not delete copies already shared.

Permissions

Jurney requests optional POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission when you enable reminders. Local notifications can include the quest of the day or pending ritual titles at your chosen time. Delivery is approximate and subject to Android battery settings; text may appear on the lock screen according to your notification settings. RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED lets Android restore scheduled reminders after restart. No exact-alarm permission is requested.

You can disable reminders in Jurney or revoke notification permission in Android settings. Jurney does not request camera, contacts, microphone or location access.

Data deletion

You can edit or delete journals and quests inside Jurney. Clearing application data or uninstalling removes private local application storage, subject to Android backup and restore settings. The publisher has no server-side copy to retrieve or delete. Copies exported, shared or backed up are controlled separately by you or their recipient.

Children's privacy

The application does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same location with a new "Last updated" date.
