Privacy Policy

Poll Up - Last updated: 2026-11-12

Poll Up is published by Nioctren. This policy covers the connected Android application and the iOS build prepared for TestFlight, also listed as “Poll Up : votes entre amis”. An account and internet connection are required. The app is intended for adults aged 18 or older. Test availability does not imply a public store release.

The current simplified build offers Google sign-in, private groups, daily votes, results, progression and a private monthly summary. The newer iOS TestFlight build also supports native Apple sign-in, tested by the publisher. Personal and weekly modes, compliments, reactions, weekly recaps and return-after-absence interfaces are temporarily hidden; hiding them does not delete existing records or disable every backend API. New icebreakers and compliments are suspended on the shared test backend. Existing records remain subject to the rules below.

Accounts and service providers

Google sign-in and Supabase process the account identifier and identity information authorized by the provider, which can include your email address and name. You choose a Poll Up nickname; your authentication email is not displayed as your group nickname. Supabase stores authentication records and the application data described below. Google and backend providers may process IP addresses and technical request information to operate and protect their services. Application requests use HTTPS.

The authentication SDK stores session credentials in private app storage. Signing out clears the local session, not your server account. Language, Light/Dark/System theme, reveal vibration and introduction-seen preferences are stored locally and remain across sign-out. Searches filter already authorized groups and members locally; search terms are not sent to the server. No offline gameplay or import of historical offline game data is provided.

Private groups and invitations

Supabase stores your nickname, account identifiers, group names, timezone, approval setting, creation timestamps, memberships, roles and ownership. Group members see each other's nicknames. Invitation records include codes, creator identifiers, expiration and revocation timestamps. Join requests include applicant identifiers, nicknames and approval status; applicants can see the invited group's name and their request status, but do not gain group membership until admitted.

The backend also stores a shared palette color, predefined icon and short group description. Owners and administrators can edit these attributes in app versions offering customization; current members can read them. No group-photo upload is added. They are deleted with the group, except original names already retained in personal receipts or reports. Do not include sensitive information in names or descriptions.

Administrators manage invitations and requests. Membership-operation receipts contain the caller, group, action and optional target identifiers for safe retries; they can survive group deletion until the caller deletes their account. Removal records prevent reuse of older invitations. Leaving, removal and demotion revoke invitations created by that person. Group deletion removes memberships, invitations, requests and removal records, but not the accounts or their earned personal progression.

Votes, results and personal progression

Daily Vote stores the group, date, timezone, question, deadlines, individual voter/target identifiers, aggregate results, nickname/identifier snapshots and title awards. While a round is open, the app returns your own choice and the total submission count, not other people's individual ballots. Completed results expose aggregate rankings and winners. In small groups, choices can still be deduced.

Departure during an open vote removes the departing person's ballot and ballots targeting them. Finalized results retain their snapshots after departure or nickname changes. Individual Daily Vote ballots are deleted at finalization after personal progression receipts are recorded. Collective history can be retained for 7, 30 or 90 days or 12 months, with 12 months as the default. Reducing the setting purges expired collective history; reads also enforce the selected cutoff. Group deletion or applicable account erasure can remove data earlier. Current members can read retained group history; departed members lose collective access.

Private progression receipts store your account, source group/round identifiers, original group name, question/date/timezone, participation and optional title. They build your counts, titles, four achievements and per-group streaks. Your global profile is private. Current group members can read only the profile progression scoped to their shared group, not your other groups. Earned receipts, including historical group names and source identifiers, survive departure and group deletion until account deletion.

Reality Check, Knowledge and Wrapped

Personal modes store question identifiers, locked subject answers and editable friend answers while open. At finalization, friends' raw answers are deleted. The retained result contains subject answers, anonymous option counts, response totals and scores, not named friend choices. Eligible participants receive private receipts; only the subject receives a personality trait when at least one friend responds. A subject leaving an open round cancels it without rewards.

Completed collective personal-mode rounds, including their subject answers and results, are deleted by maintenance when finalized more than 12 months earlier. Cancelled rounds use their deadline for the same cutoff. Group or account deletion may remove them earlier. Personal receipts, acquired titles, achievements, streaks and traits remain until account deletion; purging a collective round does not erase these acquired records or private Wrapped receipts.

Your private monthly Wrapped combines your receipts across groups by UTC finalization month and may include counts, titles, traits, archetypes and streak summaries. It is returned only to your signed-in account. Personal modes add no extra trophies; Daily Vote supplies titles and streaks.

Blocking and reports

Supabase stores the social graph of known-contact identifiers for people who shared a group, block relationships and timestamps. Contacts can remain after departure for blocking purposes. Blocking no longer requires leaving existing shared groups. It restricts direct interactions and new admission involving the relationship without automatically removing members, deleting aggregate results or notifying the other person of the reason.

Reports store reporter/target identifiers, group identifier/name, category, free-text details, timestamps, status and reviewer identifier. Reports are not anonymous: the publisher and eligible current group administrators can see the reporter's nickname and details. The reported person cannot read reports targeting them through the app, even if they are an administrator. Review does not automatically remove or block a member. Do not include passwords or unnecessary sensitive information in reports.

Reports older than 90 days are hidden from the app and deleted by daily automatic maintenance. Group deletion retains reports and the historical group name for publisher review until purge. Deleting the reporter or target account deletes the associated reports. There is no automatic moderation notification or external moderation service.

Other gameplay records and temporarily hidden features

The newer iOS TestFlight build supports native Sign in with Apple. Apple supplies an account identifier and email (including a private relay address if selected), not a full name requested by this app. A cryptographic nonce binds the identity token to authentication. A hidden email may create a separate account from an existing Google login; automatic merging must not be assumed. For Apple-linked deletion, fresh Apple authorization allows the server to exchange a one-use code, verify the linked Apple identity and revoke Poll Up access before erasure. Tokens are not persisted by that deletion function; cancellation or revocation failure prevents erasure and requires retry. This does not delete the Apple account.

Local source also prepares secondary email/password sign-in for existing accounts, including an operator-provided review account; this form is not distributed yet. Credentials are sent over HTTPS to Supabase Auth. The app does not persist or log the password and clears its field on submission or closing the form; session storage follows the SDK's usual rules. No email sign-up or password reset is offered in the app; contact support privately for operator-provided account access. Review accounts use fictional gameplay data with the same access and deletion rules as ordinary members and no moderation or administrative privileges.

Participation, streaks, rewards and receipts of viewed results are associated with your account and used to operate the game. Your global progression and monthly summary are private; shared member profiles expose only authorized group-scoped information and equipped items. Acquired records can retain original group names and source identifiers after a group is left or deleted, until account deletion.

Question packs, language, sensitivity, publication history and skip receipts support question selection without repeating recent content. Question feedback can include the account, question, group, an optional explanation and review status. Feedback is retained for up to 90 days; sensitive moderation access and actions are audited for 180 days. Moderator access does not reveal individual ballots.

Weekly modes can retain predictions, guesses and short statements. “Who Said That” deliberately reveals a submitted statement and its author to group members at the result stage; “Two Truths” reveals the statements and lie. Friends' guesses are not named. Raw weekly submissions are removed at closure after results are recorded. Account deletion removes the user's Two Truths rounds and Who Said That statements and anonymizes their other retained appearances. These interfaces are hidden in the simplified build.

Private compliments are accessible only to their recipient; saving a souvenir or displaying a profile badge is optional. Unsaved compliments expire after 90 days; saved souvenirs remain until removed or account deletion. Display consent can be withdrawn independently of saving. Existing free-text submissions can be removed with confirmation in versions exposing those controls; account deletion remains available.

Result reactions store the user's choice and aggregate counts. Weekly recaps retain aggregate snapshots; privacy erasure or shorter group retention can invalidate them. Return-after-absence features can store foreground activity and absence receipts, not an advertising analytics timeline. Their interfaces are currently hidden; backend maintenance and existing records are not automatically disabled by that change.

Notifications

Push delivery is paused and is not enabled or supported by the current iOS implementation. If enabled later in a validated Android build with permission, Firebase Cloud Messaging and Supabase process installation tokens, random device/session bindings, preferences and delivery metadata. Messages contain opaque identifiers rather than names, votes or results. Disabling notifications or signing out attempts registration revocation; network failures can delay it. Account deletion removes registrations, preferences and queued events. Events expire after 24 hours and inactive devices after 30 days when maintenance is healthy. Existing server records are not deleted merely because an interface is hidden.

Automatic maintenance and support requests

Retention maintenance runs daily at 03:00 UTC on Supabase. Project pauses or failed runs can delay physical deletion and require administrator action; the retention periods are not a guarantee of immediate deletion at the cutoff. Completed execution logs for Poll Up's maintenance jobs are cleaned after seven days by a separate daily 03:15 UTC job. This does not set the retention of provider technical logs or backups.

The publisher's operational targets are to review reports daily, check the support mailbox at least weekly, acknowledge email deletion requests within seven days, complete deletion within 30 days after ownership verification, and delete correspondence 30 days after closure unless justified retention is necessary. Email handling is manual. These targets do not override applicable legal deadlines or promise automatic completion.

Sharing chosen by you

Result images and text are generated locally and passed to the system share sheet. Default result exports omit names, group names, account/group identifiers, invitation codes and individual ballots. An explicit preview option can include Daily Vote winners' nicknames; obtain their consent before external sharing. Wrapped exports exclude friends' names. Temporary image files may remain in the system/app sharing cache until cleared.

Invitation sharing includes the code and group name; copying writes to the clipboard. Treat codes as private credentials. Poll Up does not upload these exports itself, but the system and the destination you choose may process and retain shared copies. Group/account deletion cannot retract those copies.

Account deletion and retention

In the app, use Settings > Delete my account and confirm permanent deletion. In versions with three navigation tabs, open Settings through the gear in Profile. Transfer ownership of groups with other members first; solely owned groups with no other members are deleted. You can also request account deletion without the app by private email to nioctren@gmail.com, subject to ownership verification. Never submit account details in public GitHub issues or send passwords, login codes or session tokens.

Deletion removes the Poll Up authentication account, profile, personal progression and Wrapped receipts, memberships, contacts, blocks, reports submitted by or concerning you, invitations and related requests/removal records. Personal rounds linked to your profile are deleted. Reports you reviewed lose your reviewer identifier. Other members retain their achievements. Your identity is anonymized in surviving collective Daily Vote rankings/winners while aggregate totals remain unchanged.

If authentication removal fails after preparation, a private technical deletion record remains until completion and the operation must be retried; an incomplete deletion is not success. Contact the support email for a pending request. Email requests are handled manually after verification. Account deletion does not delete your Google account, historical local data on your devices or externally shared copies. Signing out or uninstalling is not server-side deletion.

Personal receipts persist until account deletion; collective records and operation/report retention follow the rules above. User-entered group names or free text may independently mention someone and require manual review of an erasure request. Provider technical-log and backup retention periods are not yet finalized for public distribution; no maximum period or immediate erasure of those copies is promised. Contact Nioctren for the available details.

Local browser testing

A local-only browser build uses the same account/game backend for testing. Its session and language settings are stored in that browser's origin storage, separately from Android. It is not a public Web product. Native sharing and the browser's sharing/download behavior may differ.

Analytics, tracking, and advertising

Gameplay and account data are used for app functionality, authentication, safety and support, not advertising, cross-app advertising measurement or data-broker sharing. No audience analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting SDK is configured. This does not mean that no usage data is collected: participation, progression and viewed-result receipts are necessary gameplay records. Providers may process IP addresses and technical logs for service operation and security; their log and backup retention must be confirmed by the publisher. No unverified maximum retention or immediate deletion of provider copies is promised.

Permissions

Internet access allows the app to communicate with its services. The current iOS build does not request address-book, photo-library, microphone, health or location access, and does not register a push device identifier. No address-book upload, camera capture, media upload or device-advertising identifier collection is implemented. Relationships between group members are stored in the backend even though the phone's contact book is not read. Sharing uses the system destination selected by you.

Children's privacy

The connected test application is intended for adults aged 18 and over, not children. The age checkbox is a self-declaration, not independent age verification.
