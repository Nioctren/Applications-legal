Privacy Policy

Echo - Last updated: 2026-11-08

Echo is an Android application offering location-aware audio guides.

Data collection

No account is required. Echo has no app-operated backend, and the publisher does not receive your location or playback preferences.

Location

With your permission, Echo uses ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION or ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION while the application is in use to identify nearby guides. Measured positions are processed in memory on the device, never stored and never transmitted by Echo. You may refuse location permission and choose a simulated location instead.

GPS updates stop when the application goes into the background and resume when it returns to the foreground. Echo does not request background location permission. Continuing audio playback through a media service does not enable background location tracking.

Data storage and deletion

Guide content is bundled with the application. Echo stores only the selected guide and stop, speech rate, simulated location identifier and location-introduction acknowledgement in local Android preferences. It does not store measured GPS positions or record audio.

Clearing application data or uninstalling removes private local storage, subject to Android backup and restore settings. Android may include preferences in system backup or device transfer according to your device settings. Echo does not operate or have access to that backup.

Text-to-speech

Narration uses the text-to-speech engine installed on your device and the guide text bundled with Echo. Echo selects installed offline voices and rejects voices marked as network-required or not installed. Playback works offline when a suitable voice is available.

Downloading voices is an explicit action in Android settings, outside Echo. The engine and voice-download provider have their own privacy practices. Echo does not access the microphone.

External walking directions

When you request walking directions, Echo opens an external Google Maps URL with the selected destination and walking mode. Echo does not include your measured origin position. The maps application or browser may independently access location or the network under its own permissions and privacy terms.

Analytics, tracking and advertising

Echo contains no analytics, tracking, advertising or automatic data-sharing service.

Permissions and media playback

ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION are optional and used only for foreground guide discovery. FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK and WAKE_LOCK support narration through an Android media playback service, including when the screen is locked. Media controls may be visible in the notification area or on the lock screen according to Android settings. These permissions do not allow Echo to record audio or track location in the background.

You can revoke location access in Android settings, use a simulated location, or stop narration using the player or Android media controls.

Children's privacy

The publisher does not collect personal data from users, including children.
