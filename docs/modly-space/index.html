Privacy Policy

Hello Modly - Last updated: 2026-11-08

Hello Modly ("the application") is an Android application.

In short: Hello Modly works entirely offline. It makes no network requests, has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your content stays in private device storage unless you explicitly export or share it. A receiving app may use its own cloud services.

Data collection

Hello Modly has no account and no app-operated backend. The publisher does not receive your content, identifiers, analytics, advertising data, or tracking events. The publisher receives nothing at all.

Data storage

All application data is stored locally on your device using the device's private application storage. This includes the cards you add and their content - bucket lists and their items, personal journal entries, personal time counters, a totem, a personality-test or MBTI result, a chosen first name - and lightweight preferences such as a Daily Challenge completion streak or a chosen zodiac sign for the Horoscope card. The optional My Birthday card stores a month/day and, if you choose, a year to show your upcoming age. Friends' birthdays store names and month/day only. Mood words, questionnaire results, weekly reviews, saved daily pages and capsule favorites also remain local.

Shared adventures. An adventure stores up to six member names, goals, per-member progress, an outing date and a short text logbook. A local member identifier indicates who you are; it is not included in exported copies. There is no account, authenticated room or automatic synchronization. Someone receiving a copy chooses their own local member identity.

Personal journal. Journal entries can contain sensitive personal thoughts. They are written to private application storage on your device only. They are never transmitted, never analysed, and never read by the publisher.

Android backup and device transfer. The application allows Android's standard backup and device-transfer mechanisms to include its private preferences. Depending on the device and Google account settings, Android may copy your cards, lists and items, card preferences and app settings to your encrypted Google backup or directly to another Android device. This is controlled by Android and by you; Hello Modly has no account, cannot access the backup, and does not operate a cloud service. You can disable Android backup in system settings.

You can also explicitly export a JSON backup to a file you choose or to the Android clipboard. Clipboard content can be read by other apps, so file export is recommended for sensitive content. Hello Modly never uploads a manual export.

Data transmission

The application makes no network requests. Every piece of daily content - quotes, words of the day, historical notes, positive thoughts, horoscope readings, challenges and ideas - is selected locally on your device from content shipped inside the application. No user data is transmitted to any external server by Modly, including the publisher's. Explicit exports and sharing may pass a copy to an app or destination you select; that app's transmission and retention practices are outside Modly's control.

Analytics, tracking, and advertising

The application does not use analytics, tracking, advertising identifiers, or advertising services.

Data sharing

The publisher does not sell data or receive your content. At your explicit request, Modly can display an adventure QR code, copy its JSON to the clipboard, save it to a chosen file, or hand a file to the Android share chooser. These copies contain member names, goals, progress, outing date and notes. Share only with people you trust. Anyone with a copy can read, retain and forward it; deleting your local adventure does not recall exported copies.

The app you choose for sharing may upload or store the file under its own privacy policy. Android may also process private preferences through the encrypted system backup or device transfer described above, under your own Android and Google account settings.

Permissions

The standard Internet permission remains declared but unused by active cards. Modly requests no location, contacts or microphone access.

Camera (optional). Camera access is requested only when you open the adventure QR scanner. Frames are processed on the device using a bundled scanner; Modly does not save images or upload camera frames. You can deny access and import a JSON file instead.

Notifications (optional). Reminders are off by default. Opting in requests Android notification permission. Modly schedules friends' birthday reminders the day before at 9 am and a Sunday review at 6 pm when the corresponding cards are enabled. Times are approximate and subject to Android battery settings. The boot-completed permission restores scheduled reminders after a restart; no exact-alarm permission is used. Notification text can include a friend's name and may appear according to your Android lock-screen settings. Opting out or erasing app data cancels reminders.

Children's privacy

The application does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Your choices and rights

You can view and edit all your data and preferences directly in the application. You can export your data to a file; clipboard export is available as a warned fallback because other apps may read clipboard content. Hello Modly never uploads a manual export.

You can revoke camera or notification access in Android settings. You can opt out of reminders inside Modly. Adventure sharing is always an explicit action, not an automatic background transfer.

You can delete your data and application preferences from Settings > Backup > Erase all data. Uninstalling removes local application storage, subject to Android backup and restore settings. You can disable or delete Android backups through Android or Google account controls where available.

Because Hello Modly has no account and no app-operated backend, the publisher holds no copy of your data and therefore cannot retrieve, correct, or delete local content or Android backups it cannot access. Use the contact page below for privacy questions concerning data processed by the publisher.

Retention and security

Local data remains until you edit, erase, uninstall, or restore it, subject to Android backup settings. The application uses Android private application storage and validates backup documents, but no software or device storage can be guaranteed absolutely secure. Prefer file export over clipboard for sensitive free text.

Privacy by design

Hello Modly minimizes data, permissions, and network access. Future external APIs, AI, cloud services, analytics, advertising, authentication, precise location, or sensitive permissions require a new privacy review and updated disclosure before release. The design follows the principles in the CNIL mobile application recommendations.
